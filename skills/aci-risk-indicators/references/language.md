# Language

ACI output is quantitative risk analytics. Present it in the vocabulary below, and without the words the ACI Legal Compass excludes.

## Use

- ACI Risk Indicator, and "indicator value" for the `score` field
- risk band
- quantitative risk analytics
- model-derived
- scenario output
- independent analysis
- professional or institutional user
- digital assets

## Avoid

The ACI Legal Compass (§5.3) lists these, and Aethon's release checks reject them:

<!-- prohibited-vocabulary:start -->
- recommendation, recommend
- should, must, consider buying, consider selling
- rating, rated
- credit opinion
- advice, advise
- crypto, cryptocurrency (say "digital assets")
- predicts, forecasts
- guaranteed, certain
- outperform, underperform
<!-- prohibited-vocabulary:end -->

Quote ACI's disclosures and the classification anchor exactly as the tools return them.
